Physical Mechanism: Heavy-Ion Triggered SEGR in Silicon Carbide

Plasma filament electrodynamics, transient oxide polarization, and non-linear $(Q, C_{gd})$ phase trajectory shifts.

Single-Event Gate Rupture (SEGR) is catastrophic for wide-bandgap (SiC/GaN) MOSFETs in deep-space environments. When a relativistic cosmic heavy ion strikes the reverse-biased n-drift region, it creates an ultra-dense cylindrical electron-hole plasma track. Under high drain bias $V_{ds}$, holes drift rapidly toward the $\text{SiO}_2/\text{SiC}$ interface. The accumulated trapped displacement charge $Q$ magnifies the local electric field across the 45 nm gate dielectric beyond its intrinsic breakdown strength ($E_{ox} > 10\text{ MV/cm}$), culminating in irreversible dielectric vaporization.

SECTION 1 // ELECTRODYNAMICS

Dielectric Field Amplification & Poisson-Continuity

The instantaneous electric field $E_{ox}(t)$ across the gate oxide is driven by the superposition of the applied gate bias and the transient image charge induced by the plasma filament:

$$E_{ox}(t) = \frac{\varepsilon_{SiC}}{\varepsilon_{ox}} \left[ \frac{V_{ds}(t) - V_{g}(t) - \Delta \Phi_{ms}}{W_{epi}} \right] + \frac{Q_{trap}(t)}{\varepsilon_{ox} A_{spot}}$$

Where $\varepsilon_{SiC} \approx 9.7 \varepsilon_0$, $\varepsilon_{ox} \approx 3.9 \varepsilon_0$, $W_{epi}$ is the drift epitaxial thickness ($30\,\mu\text{m}$), and $Q_{trap}$ is the total primary trapped ion displacement charge.

Critical Breakdown Limit: $E_{crit} \ge 10.0\text{ MV/cm}$ for thermal $\text{SiO}_2$.
SECTION 2 // SOLID STATE KINETICS

Plasma Filament Track & Linear Energy Transfer (LET)

Electron-hole pairs generated along the primary ion track obey dense cylindrical ambipolar diffusion coupled with field-assisted drift:

$$G_{eh}(r, z) = \frac{\text{LET} \cdot \rho_{SiC}}{E_{eh}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi} r_0} \exp\left( -\frac{r^2}{2 r_0^2} \right)$$

With electron-hole creation energy $E_{eh} = 9.0\text{ eV}$ in 4H-SiC, track core radius $r_0 \approx 50\text{ nm}$, and material density $\rho_{SiC} = 3.21\text{ g/cm}^3$. At cryogenic lunar night temperatures ($T = 40\text{ K}$), reduced phonon scattering increases carrier mobility $\mu_p(T) \propto T^{-2.2}$, accelerating hole accumulation at the oxide interface.

Cryogenic Trap Lifetime: $\tau_{trap}(40\text{ K}) > 10^3 \times \tau_{trap}(300\text{ K})$.

$$C_{gd}(V_{ds}) = \frac{A_{gd}}{\sqrt{\frac{2(V_{ds} + V_{bi})}{q N_d \varepsilon_{SiC}}} + \frac{t_{ox}}{\varepsilon_{ox}}} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \Delta Q_{trap} = \oint_{T_{sw}} \left[ i_g(t) - C_{gd,0} \frac{d V_{ds}}{dt} \right] dt$$

Ultrafast Gate Drive & Bus Voltage Intercept

The closed-loop mitigation controller executes an autonomous state-machine that safely quenches the catastrophic breakdown condition:

$$V_{ds,\text{safe}} = \left[ 1 - \alpha \cdot \mathcal{H}\left( \frac{\partial C_{gd}}{\partial Q} - \kappa_{crit} \right) \right] V_{ds,\text{nominal}}$$
